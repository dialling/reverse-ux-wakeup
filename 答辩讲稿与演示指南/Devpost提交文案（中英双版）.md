## Inspiration

> **"Every extra minute you stay in bed, you are personally buying braised drumsticks for your roommates."**

Why do college students set seven alarms (`06:50`, `06:53`, `06:55`, `07:00`) and still sleep straight through an 8:00 AM midterm?

Because modern alarm apps worship **frictionless UX**. Giant snooze buttons and silky-smooth swipe gestures empower **spinal-cord muscle memory**: your thumb dismisses the alarm before your prefrontal cortex even wakes up.

Meanwhile, most "Reverse UX" experiments fail a fatal product test: if an app is merely annoying (like a broken volume slider), users curse once and uninstall it within 30 seconds. Conversely, stranger-based cash-pool habit apps demand massive cold-start traffic and suffer from rampant cheating.

We built **Rise & Fine (*醒醒！分钱了*)** for the **Reverse UX Challenge** by fusing **deliberately hostile cognitive friction** with the smallest, highest-density social-proof unit on earth: **a 4-person college dorm room ("Room 404")**. When four roommates lock CNY 50.00 each into a closed CNY 200.00 morning pact at 11:30 PM, two forces make the app impossible to abandon:
1. **Zero-Cold-Start Peer Pressure**: You sleep three feet away from the people who hear your alarm. Uninstalling or rage-quitting means instant public surrender.
2. **10x Immediate Loss Aversion**: Losing $1 to a faceless platform barely stings; watching your cash fly across the room in real time to buy extra breakfast drumsticks for your smug upper-bunk roommate hurts ten times more.

## What it does

**Rise & Fine** turns waking up in **Room 404** (*Grinder Zhang* in Bed 1, *8-AM Qiang* in Bed 2, *Relapse Li* in Bed 3, and *Coma Wei* in Bed 4) into a real-time, zero-sum social battlefield:

### 1. Closed-Loop 4-Roommate Deposit Pact (`CNY 200.00`)
Every night at `23:30`, four roommates lock **CNY 50.00** (`5,000` cents) each into a **CNY 200.00** (`20,000` cents) shared contract pool with zero platform rake. Every penalty lost by a sleepy roommate flows directly into the balances of awake roommates, while an **Early-Bird Jackpot** awards accumulated pre-dawn penalties to the first roommate who clears morning verification.

### 2. Five Maddening, Yet 100% Deterministically Solvable, Anti-UX Gauntlets
Dismissing the `07:00` alarm or begging for extra sleep requires surviving five anti-muscle-memory mechanisms:
- **Gauntlet 1: Evasive Slider & 9.8G Bed-Gravity Snapback**: Lunging blindly at the wake-up slider makes it dodge away across the track. You must hold a `0.8s` anti-sleepwalking safety pin first, push steadily within a narrow safe-speed band (`1.5s–3.0s`), and hold at the finish (`>=98%`) for `0.5s`. Flick too fast (`>105%/s`) and you derail for a **CNY 0.50** fine; let go early and **9.8G reverse gravity** yanks your progress straight back to zero.
- **Gauntlet 2: Paste-Blocked Humiliation Confession Typing**: Want 5 more minutes of sleep? `Ctrl+V`, `Cmd+V`, drag-and-drop, context menus, and clipboard bursts are strictly blocked (attempting to paste swaps in a harsher confession!). You must manually type a ~40-character self-roasting dorm confession, including exact Chinese full-width punctuation. **A single typo wipes the entire input**, while every `10s` of hesitation bleeds **CNY 1.00** live to awake roommates.
- **Gauntlet 3: Roommate Ambush Raid & Oscillating Shuffled Keypad**: Awake roommates can launch a **CNY 10.00 Ambush Raid** on anyone suspected of relapsing into bed. The target's screen erupts with an alarm and two-digit arithmetic problems on a keypad that **continuously sways in a 2.3s sine wave AND Fisher-Yates shuffles digits `0–9` after every single keystroke**. Solve 2 problems before countdown or 4 strikes to survive; fail and CNY 10.00 splits among awake roommates.
- **Gauntlet 4: High-Voltage Flow Connect (`5x5` / `6x6`)**: Pair all matching colored electrodes across the grid without crossing wires within 30–60s. Brushing against another color triggers a **Double-Circuit Blowout**, wiping *both* your active wire and the innocent wire you touched while fining **CNY 0.50**.
- **Gauntlet 5: Zero-Tolerance Scooter Jam (`6x6`)**: Slide blocking campus e-scooters along their axes to escort your red breakfast scooter out the `EXIT` gate. Even a slight scrape triggers a **Dorm Warden Collision Alert**, snapping the scooter back to its pre-drag origin and fining **CNY 0.50**.

### 3. Multi-Device LAN QR Sync & Three Standalone Views
Alongside full-screen mobile QR join links over local Wi-Fi/Hotspot, **Rise & Fine** ships with three zero-dependency, double-click standalone HTML editions: **4-Phone Live Sync View (`?view=quad`)**, **Single-Phone Personal View (`?view=single`)**, and **Room 404 Contract Console (`?view=director`)**.

## How we built it

- **Frontend & Visual Architecture**: Built with **React 19**, **TypeScript 5.7**, **Vite 6**, and **Tailwind CSS v4** in a high-contrast Neo-Brutalist palette (`#FACC15` hazard yellow, `#EF4444` 8-AM alarm red, and `#0F172A` contract slate) designed to jolt sleepy retinas awake.
- **Exact-Cent Pure State Machine (`dormEngine.ts`)**: All monetary transitions operate exclusively on integer cents (`balanceCents`, `publicPoolCents`). Every state transition (hesitation bleed ticks, collision fines, ambush settlements, early-bird jackpots, and remainder-safe integer splits) runs a runtime conservation assertion:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} B_i(t) + P_{\text{pool}}(t) \equiv 20000 \text{ cents } (\text{CNY } 200.00), \quad \forall t \ge 0, \quad B_i(t) \ge 0
$$

- **Algorithmic Solvability Solvers (`verify-puzzles.mjs`)**: Hostile UX must never degenerate into broken soft-locks. Every built-in puzzle board is verified by zero-dependency solvers:
  - *Flow Connect* levels (`5x5` 5-color, `6x6` 5-color, `6x6` 6-color) are proven 100% full-grid solvable via **Depth-First Search (DFS)** backtracking.
  - *Scooter Jam* levels (`6x6`) are verified via **Breadth-First Search (BFS)** over vehicle coordinate state vectors, calibrating exact shortest paths to a 30–60 second human wake-up window:

$$
d_{\text{BFS}}^{*}(S_0, S_{\text{exit}}) \in \{10, 12, 14\} \text{ moves}
$$

- **Dual-Mode Real-Time Sync (`syncServer.mjs` + `syncClient.ts`)**: A lightweight **Node.js HTTP + WebSocket (`ws`)** server auto-detects LAN IPv4 addresses, renders per-bed **QR codes (`qrcode`)**, and broadcasts revision-checked state transitions across phones in milliseconds, seamlessly falling back to an in-browser bus when opened offline via `file://`.
- **AI-Assisted Visual Pipeline**: Our competition poster (`2400x3600` PNG & single-page vector PDF) was bootstrapped via a 3-node **Dify Workflow DSL** (`Start -> LLM -> End`, compatible with `deepseek-v4-pro` and `AMD Free Token Factory`), then refined into layered, editable HTML/SVG vector artwork.

## Challenges we ran into

1. **Separating Deliberate Frustration from Unfair Input Bugs**: Early builds of the *Humiliation Confession Typer* wiped the input box mid-syllable during Chinese Pinyin typing because raw `onChange` fired intermediate ASCII letters before character selection. Implementing `compositionstart` / `compositionend` IME lifecycle tracking ensured Pinyin candidates never falsely trigger a wipe, so every reset is 100% earned by an actual typo.
2. **Zero-Cent Drift Under Concurrent Multi-Phone Fines**: When Bed 2 bleeds CNY 1.00 (`100` cents) across 3 awake roommates (`100 = 33 * 3 + 1` cent remainder) at the exact moment Bed 1 settles a CNY 10.00 raid, naive floating-point math breaks conservation. We built deterministic quotient-and-remainder integer distribution with optimistic revision rebasing (`REBASE_REQUIRED`), verified across **1,500 randomized fuzz steps** in **Vitest**.
3. **Zero-Server `file://` Portability**: Standard Vite ES module builds fail with CORS errors when double-clicked directly from Finder or Explorer without a local HTTP server. We engineered a custom inline IIFE bundler (`build-standalone.mjs`) so judges can open any of the three views directly from disk with zero setup.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of

- **100% Product-Logic Self-Consistency**: Every ounce of user hatred directly measures how effectively **Rise & Fine** is firing up their prefrontal cortex and saving their 8:00 AM attendance.
- **Mathematical Rigor Behind Playful Chaos**: Pairing **1,500-step randomized money-conservation fuzzing** with **DFS/BFS puzzle solvability proofs** guarantees zero soft-locks, zero negative balances, and exact cent-level accounting.
- **Instant Multi-Phone Dorm Theater**: Tapping *Launch Ambush Raid* on Bed 1's phone and watching Bed 3's screen erupt into a swaying, reshuffling arithmetic siren in under `500ms` turns a mundane alarm into unforgettable live comedy.

## What we learned

Building **Reverse UX** taught us that breaking usability conventions actually demands *stricter* UX engineering than conventional smooth design. In normal apps, users forgive minor ambiguity; in high-punishment interfaces, if a user cannot immediately tell *why* they were fined or *how* to succeed on the next try, productive friction collapses into learned helplessness. Crisp visual telemetry (our live safe-velocity gauge on the Evasive Slider, next-character highlight in the Confession Typer, and 1-cell nudge controls in Scooter Jam) is what transforms frustration into wide-awake mastery.

## What's next for Rise & Fine

- **Accelerometer Proof-of-Verticality**: Requiring upper-bunk roommates to physically climb down the ladder and walk 15 verified steps before final morning settlement unlocks.
- **Campus Canteen Drumstick Vouchers**: Partnering with university breakfast stalls so Room 404's weekly zero-sum ledger converts directly into QR vouchers for real soy milk and braised drumsticks.
- **Procedural Daily Puzzles with Live BFS Gating**: Synthesizing fresh *Flow Connect* and *Scooter Jam* boards every morning in a Web Worker, gated by our BFS solver so every board is guaranteed solvable in \\( 10 \le d^{*} \le 16 \\) moves.

## Inspiration

> **“你多睡的每一分钟，都在亲手给室友加鸡腿。”**

为什么当代大学生手机里设了七八个闹钟（`06:50`、`06:53`、`06:55`），最后还是在早八点名那一刻从被窝里惊坐而起？

因为市面上所有优秀的传统闹钟都在奉行**“顺从式 UX”**：贪睡按钮越做越大、右滑关闭越做越丝滑。结果大脑皮层还在做梦，大拇指已经凭**脊髓反射与肌肉记忆**把闹钟盲划关掉了。

然而，做一款让人“讨厌但不得不用”的逆向 UX 产品面临双重死穴：如果只是单纯把界面做得难用，用户骂完第一句就会立刻卸载；而传统的陌生人早起打卡分钱平台又高度依赖百万级日活冷启动与外部补贴。

我们为 **CrossPointX 2026 逆向 UX 挑战赛** 打造了 **《醒醒！分钱了》（Rise & Fine）**，将**刻意制造认知阻力的逆向交互**锁定在全球最小、博弈密度最高的熟人单元——**一间 4 人大学寝室（“404 寝室”）**：
1. **零外部流量冷启动**：同屋 4 位室友睡前各锁入 `¥50.00` 组成 `¥200.00` 封闭契约池即可闭环运转；同住一屋听得到闹钟，谁敢卸载谁就是全寝公认认怂王。
2. **10 倍放大的即时损失厌恶**：丢掉几块钱给陌生平台也许不心疼，但亲眼看着自己的早饭钱化作红包飞进上铺室友碗里变成双份卤鸡腿，杀伤力直接拉满。

## What it does

**Rise & Fine（《醒醒！分钱了》）** 围绕 **404 寝室四人角色群像**（1号床·卷王老张、2号床·早八阿强、3号床·回笼觉小李、4号床·死睡阿伟），构建了完整的早起互坑与零和分钱飞轮：

### 1. 404 寝室 `¥200.00` 封闭零和契约池
每晚 `23:30` 四人各注入 `¥50.00`（`5000` 分），零平台抽成。赖床、手滑、犹豫产生的每一分罚金都会实时打给已起床室友；若尚无室友完成核验，罚金滚入公共待分奖池，由**首位通关起床的室友触发「早起头彩」全额独揽**。

### 2. 五重令人抓狂但 100% 确定可通关的逆向 UX 关卡
想要关掉 `07:00` 警报或妄想贪睡，必须直面五道专治闭眼盲按的反肌肉记忆关卡：
- **关卡一：躲避手指滑块 + 9.8G 万有引力倒退**：未解锁鲁莽盲扑时，滑块会沿轨道随机闪避弹开并贴脸嘲讽；必须先长按防梦游安全栓满 `0.8 秒` 解锁，再按绿色安全速率区间在 `1.5–3.0 秒` 内匀速推至终点驻留 `0.5 秒`。猛甩超速脱轨立扣 `¥0.50`，中途松手则以 **9.8G 被窝万有引力** 急速倒吸归零。
- **关卡二：禁粘贴手打社死认怂贪睡**：想再眯 5 分钟？系统彻底拦截 `Ctrl+V` / `Cmd+V`、拖拽与剪贴板突发输入（企图粘贴作弊直接惩罚切换更羞耻的宣言重打）。必须含中文全角标点一字不差手打约 40 字寝室认怂宣言；**打错任意一字瞬间整段清空重打**，且每犹豫满 `10 秒` 自动滴血划扣 `¥1.00` 实时打给已起室友。
- **关卡三：室友突袭查岗 + 正弦晃动洗牌算术**：已起床室友可随时对疑似回笼觉室友发起 `¥10.00` 限时突袭。被查岗者屏幕警报爆闪，数字键盘不仅全程以 `2.3 秒` 周期**正弦波摇摆**，而且**每按下一位数字或退格键，`0–9` 键位当场 Fisher-Yates 随机洗牌重排**。连续算对 2 道两位数算术题方可保本。
- **关卡四：30–60 秒「同色小球不交叉连线」清醒大考**：在 `5x5` / `6x6` 配电箱网格中两两接通全部同色电极，严禁路径交叉；一旦误触异色线路立即触发**“触电短路双崩”**，当前线与被撞无辜线路双双清空并扣罚 `¥0.50`。
- **关卡五：30–60 秒「无碰撞挪车出库」清醒大考**：在 `6x6` 早高峰车棚平移违停电瓶车护送红色【早八驴】驶出右侧 `EXIT` 出口；全程零刮擦容忍，轻微擦碰邻车或墙壁立即触发宿管咆哮警报、**车辆弹回拖动前原位**并扣罚 `¥0.50`。

### 3. 局域网真机扫码联机与三套免安装直开视图
内置轻量局域网同步服务支持 4 部真机扫码实时互抢红包；同时提供 **`醒醒分钱了-4部联机手机同屏版.html`**、**`醒醒分钱了-单部手机版.html`** 与 **`醒醒分钱了-寝室总控台版.html`** 三套零环境依赖双击即玩单文件成品。

## How we built it

- **前端交互与高反差工业风视觉**：基于 **React 19** + **TypeScript 5.7** + **Vite 6** + **Tailwind CSS v4** 构建，采用 `#FACC15` 防爆警戒黄、`#EF4444` 早八警报红与 `#0F172A` 契约深灰高对比设计语言。
- **精确到“分”的纯函数资金守恒引擎 (`dormEngine.ts`)**：底层全部采用整数“分”运算，任意单机或局域网多端并发事件均强制执行运行时守恒断言：

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} B_i(t) + P_{\text{pool}}(t) \equiv 20000 \text{ cents } (\text{CNY } 200.00), \quad \forall t \ge 0, \quad B_i(t) \ge 0
$$

- **独立数学求解器验证 100% 可通关 (`verify-puzzles.mjs`)**：全部内置关卡均通过零依赖算法求解器离线及自动化测试双重验算：
  - 「同色连线」经 **DFS 回溯路径求解器** 严格证明 100% 存在互不交叉满格解；
  - 「无碰撞挪车」经 **BFS 状态空间最短路求解器** 精确标定最优步数，锁定清醒人类 30–60 秒黄金通关窗口：

$$
d_{\text{BFS}}^{*}(S_0, S_{\text{exit}}) \in \{10, 12, 14\} \text{ moves}
$$

- **HTTP + WebSocket 双模实时同步层**：基于 **Node.js + `ws` + `qrcode`** 自动探测局域网 IPv4 并生成 4 张床位专属二维码，毫秒级广播乐观锁状态版本；离线双击 `file://` 打开时自动无感切换至本地联机总线。
- **Dify AI 海报生成与精修管线**：构建 3 节点 **Dify Workflow DSL**（`dify-amd-poster-workflow.yml`），真实调用大模型节点生成 HTML/SVG 海报初稿，再对齐实机账本参数进行矢量分层精修导出 `2400x3600` PNG 与单页满版 PDF。

## Challenges we ran into

1. **中文拼音输入法（IME）与“错一字全清”的精确兼容**：早期测试中，敲击中文拼音字母上屏前会触发浏览器中间态 `onChange`，导致拼音字母被误判为错别字而瞬间清空。接入 `compositionstart` / `compositionend` 生命周期锁后，只有真正上屏的汉字与全角标点才进入逐字校验，确保每一次惩罚都 100% 公平可信。
2. **多端并发扣罚下的余数守恒难题**：当 `1.00` 元（`100` 分）罚金在 3 名清醒室友间实时平分（`100 = 33 * 3 + 1`）且并发叠加 `¥10.00` 突袭结算时，极易产生账目漂移。我们设计了确定性整数商余分配算法与版本号冲突重放机制，并通过 **Vitest 1,500 步随机并发 Fuzz 测试** 验证零误差、零负数穿仓。
3. **`file://` 协议免服务器双击直开打包**：标准 Vite ES Module 产物在直接双击打开时会因浏览器 `file://` CORS 策略白屏。我们编写了单文件 IIFE 内联打包脚本 `build-standalone.mjs`，无需启动任何本地服务即可双击畅玩全部三套视图。

## Accomplishments that we're proud of

- **“讨厌度 = 清醒度”的产品体验闭环**：当用户对着重力归零滑块和晃动洗牌键盘咬牙切齿时，前额叶皮层已经彻底开机，反人类阻力精准转化为早八出勤率。
- **确定性数学底座支撑戏剧化交互**：以 **1,500 步随机资金守恒 Fuzz 单测** 与 **DFS/BFS 独立关卡求解器** 托底，真正做到“过程抓狂崩溃、规则透明可解、账本分毫不差”。

## What we learned

逆向 UX 实践让我们深刻意识到：**设计“好用的难用”，比设计常规顺滑界面需要更严苛的反馈清晰度**。在高惩罚交互中，必须通过实时安全速率仪表、下一字符高亮提示与 1 格微调按钮让用户瞬间看懂“为什么受罚”与“下次如何凭实力过关”，才能把挫败感转化为斗志而非习得性无助。

## What's next for Rise & Fine

- **手机陀螺仪“下床步态核验”**：结合移动端加速度计与陀螺仪步态特征，要求上铺室友真实爬下梯子走满 15 步后才解锁最终保本结算。
- **高校食堂早餐档口联动**：将 404 寝室每周五的零和清算单直接兑换为校园食堂豆浆与卤鸡腿核销码。
- **Web Worker 每日动态关卡生成与 BFS 门控**：每天清晨动态合成全新连线与挪车棋盘，由浏览器端 BFS 求解器实时保证最优步数落在 \\( 10 \le d^{*} \le 16 \\) 步黄金区间。
